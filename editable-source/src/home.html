06 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS+

Bring us
the messy part.

What does forward-deployed engineering mean? {{icon:plus}}

An engineer works closely with your business team, learns the operation, and builds alongside the people using the software. It connects discovery, development, and adoption in one working relationship.

Do we have to replace all our current tools? {{icon:plus}}

We first look at what you already have. We improve, connect, or extend systems where they fit, and recommend a replacement only where it supports the agreed business outcome.

How is vertical SaaS different from a custom build? {{icon:plus}}

Vertical SaaS focuses on the shared workflows of a particular industry. A custom build addresses a process specific to your organisation. We assess repeatability, ownership, integrations, and support before choosing the approach.

Can we start with one workflow? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. A five-day proof of concept or a focused integration sprint gives you a useful starting point. We agree on scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and the next step before the build begins.

What happens after launch? {{icon:plus}}

We plan testing, documentation, training, and handover with the build. Ongoing support and improvements can be scoped around the workflows and systems your team needs to sustain.
