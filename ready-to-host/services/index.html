08 / QUESTIONS CLIENTS ASK+

Straight answers.

Do you only work with one platform?

No. We design around your whole stack. The right answer might be a business suite such as Zoho One, a connection to the CRM or accounting system you already have, a mixed stack, or a focused custom build.

Can you help if our current CRM is messy?

Yes. An audit shows what is wrong with the data model, pipelines, permissions, automation and reports, and gives you a prioritised plan. The platform does not always need replacing.

Do we need AI for automation?

No. Predictable rules are usually better handled by ordinary automation. AI helps when a task needs interpretation or handles a lot of variation. High-impact decisions keep human control.

Will AI be allowed to change records automatically?

That is a design decision based on risk. Some low-risk, reversible actions can be automated after testing. Financial, contractual or customer-facing actions usually need explicit approval. Permissions, logs and fallbacks are defined before anything goes live.

How do we know if the project succeeded?

We agree a baseline and a few process measures up front: cycle time, manual hours, errors, data completeness, reporting time or adoption. Results are reviewed against that baseline, in context.

How quickly can we start?

Usually with a five-day proof of concept. A clear quote and sign-off follow within about three days, and the project kicks off within a week of sign-off.
