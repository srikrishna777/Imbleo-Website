03 / PRACTICAL AI

AI is not a coat of paint.

We map how the work really happens, re-engineer it, then decide which steps need rules, AI or a person.

Not “where can we add an agent?” but “which outcome needs to improve, and what is the simplest safe way to improve it?”

Every step in a workflow meets these four questions, in order:

  1. 01Does this step need to exist?No Remove itDrop duplicate entry, extra approvals and needless hand-offs.
  2. 02Is the rule predictable?Yes Automate with rulesOrdinary automation for fixed if/then logic.
  3. 03Does it need judgement?Yes Use an AI agentAI for messy inputs, ambiguity and interpretation.
  4. 04Is the decision consequential?Yes A person approvesPeople approve money, commitments and anything irreversible.

Otherwise, the step moves on to the next question.
