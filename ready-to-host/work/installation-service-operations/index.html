Tasks are not jobs

The business kept customers in Zoho CRM and ran day-to-day work as tasks in Zoho Projects.

Tasks did not show the job as a whole: what it needs, when, and where it stands.

What the team needed

Job-level view

Materials, hours, visits, due dates and PO details in one place.

A real calendar

A visual schedule that shows job status.

Clear hand-offs

Stages, owners and notifications between departments.

Consistent contracts

Agreements generated from templates and signed online.

Before and after

BEFORE
  • CRM holds client details only
  • Work organised as project tasks
  • Job details hard to see in one view
  • Hand-offs depend on memory
AFTER
  • Separate Client and Job records
  • Jobs hold the details needed to deliver
  • Blueprints guide stages and hand-offs
  • A calendar widget shows the schedule

Documents, signed

Agreements are generated from CRM data and sent for e-signature.

  • Mail-merge templates for service agreements and contracts
  • Sent through Zoho Sign
  • Staff update jobs from the CRM mobile app
  • Estimates, quotes and invoices stay in Zoho Books, by design

What changed

Delivered / observed

  • Job-level information in one structured place
  • A visual schedule for the installation team
  • Clearer stages and hand-offs
  • Job updates from the field
  • Repeatable document signing

No measured results were supplied, so outcomes are described qualitatively.

Model the work, not just the customer

A CRM becomes an operations tool once the job, not the contact, is the thing you track.
