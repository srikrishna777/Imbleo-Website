Not just moving records to a new CRM

The sales team had run on Salesforce for years. The move to Zoho had a tight deadline.

The goal was a better day-to-day experience: the right fields at the right time, no missed follow-ups, and units you can actually see.

What needed fixing

Long forms

Users searched through screens to find the fields each stage needed.

Missed follow-ups

Calls, reminders and next dates had no consistent path.

One person, many units

A buyer could ask about several apartments. Each interest had to stay distinct.

Hidden inventory

Checking if a unit was available meant asking someone.

Manual cost sheets

Excel templates per project, 15 to 30 minutes each.

A guided lead lifecycle

Conceptual flow. Each step asks only for what that stage needs.

  1. 01Lead captured

    web, call, walk-in or campaign

  2. 02Find or create

    duplicate check, source recorded

  3. 03Follow up

    outcome and next date recorded

  4. 04Site visit

    booked, confirmation sent

  5. 05Qualify

    hand over to sales

  6. 06Opportunity

    with project and unit context

What changed

Delivered / observed

  • Cost sheets generated and sent in under a minute, down from 15 to 30 minutes
  • Stage-specific data capture through Blueprints
  • Unit availability and buyer interest in one visual workflow
  • Follow-ups and customer messages tied to the lead lifecycle
  • Shared sales and management reports in Zoho Analytics
