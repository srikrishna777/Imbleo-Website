Files alone do not tell you what is about to expire

A compliance-heavy supply and installation business needed to track product documents with expiry or renewal dates.

The key idea: separate where a document is stored from the register that tracks who owns it and what to do next.

Proof-of-concept scope

Small on purpose. Reminders come later, once the data is trusted.

  • Register kept in CRM, no large new system
  • Spot current, expired, missing and renewal-due documents
  • Store a trusted link, not duplicate files
  • Future: a Product then Document Type folder structure
