Many products, many languages

An international equipment distributor already used Zoho CRM. Orders came from its own custom website.

Each order needed the right document and emails, based on product, location and language.

What needed solving

Website to CRM

A custom order page, not a standard form, had to feed CRM reliably.

Right document

Different products need different paperwork.

Right language

Location and language change what the customer receives.

"Where is my order?"

Customers had to ask the team for status.

From website order to customer inbox

Logical flow. Unsubscribe and data-quality rules sit alongside the automation layer.

Two questions decide every message

Which product configuration applies? Which language or region applies? Both feed the document and email choice.

  • Product keys match orders to the right product data
  • Templates and rules work together, not just translated copy
  • Custom unsubscribe handling built into the sequence
  • Flagged customer records handled by the automation
