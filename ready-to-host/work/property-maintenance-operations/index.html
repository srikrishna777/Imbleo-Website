Tracked, but not connected

A multi-service property maintenance business ran on Smartsheet, Excel estimate templates, Outlook and Xero.

Each step meant re-entering or passing information along. The team needed a joined-up process, not another tracker.

Where work got repeated

Separate tracker

Enquiries, jobs and schedules lived apart from finance and email.

Spreadsheet estimates

Several templates, edited by hand each time.

Quotes typed twice

Accounts recreated each estimate as a quote in Xero.

Updates by email

Job news travelled through Outlook threads.

Before and after

BEFORE
  • Smartsheet tracker
  • Excel estimate, edited by hand
  • Spreadsheet emailed to accounts
  • Quote recreated in Xero
  • Updates relayed through Outlook
AFTER
  • Website or staff enquiry into Zoho CRM
  • Date booked from the record
  • Estimate built in Zoho Creator
  • Quote and invoices sent from Zoho Books
  • Invoice created in Xero via Zapier

Every tool has one job

Books is the customer-facing front end. Xero stays the accounting back end. Not a full two-way sync.

What changed

Delivered / observed

  • One operational record instead of a standalone tracker
  • Website enquiries checked against existing records
  • Dates checked and booked from the job record
  • Estimates entered once and turned into quotes
  • Deposit and final invoicing simplified
  • Job reports generated from templates
  • Xero kept as the accounting back end

No measured results were supplied, so outcomes are described qualitatively.

Lighter can be better

We considered a full field-service platform. A CRM-led flow with a few targeted tools fit the team better.
