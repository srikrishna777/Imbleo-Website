Sensitive data, lots of paperwork

A specialist advice practice needs detailed financial and medical information before it can help a client.

It also needs signed authorities to ask each existing insurer about current policies. Often several insurers per client.

What made it slow

Re-keying

Fact-find and medical answers had to be moved into the CRM by hand.

Scattered profile

Assets, debts, income, expenses and policies were hard to see together.

One document per insurer

Each provider needed its own authority to enquire.

Chasing

Incomplete forms needed follow-up, and some clients needed help filling them in.

From fact-find to signed authority

The core workflow. Incomplete forms trigger reminders; the team can also complete forms with the client.

What changed

Delivered / observed

  • Fact-find to authority workflow in about 17 minutes, from 2 to 3 days
  • Less re-entry from forms into CRM
  • A structured view of each client’s finances and policies
  • Provider-specific authorities generated from submitted details
  • Signed documents stored on the client record
  • Automated reminders for outstanding forms

The CRM supports the advisers’ process and record keeping. It does not make advice decisions.

Collect once, use everywhere

When intake is structured, documents, signatures and follow-ups can build themselves from the same data.
